## Timeout assessment

Ordinary cycle: nominal sum preflight 12 + claim 20 + authorize 20 + command 45 + result 20 = 117s (before local overhead). Existing local 80s subprocess watchdog can expire before all nominal per-operation budgets are exhausted. The remote exec maximum remains 45s; the 80s local child watchdog is a separate mechanism, not permission to increase MCP timeouts. Timeout after claim but before persisted receipt remains ambiguous; never re-claim or replay. Timeout with valid result_ready may only redeliver the saved result.

Candidate receipt adapter currently runs in monitor process: preflight 12 + POST 20 each attempt, three attempts and delays 1+3 = 100s nominal; this is not an absolute end-to-end maximum. DNS, TLS and slow reads may exceed per-socket inactivity bounds; cooperative stop does not interrupt an active receipt call. Local synthetic socketpair fixture timeout 0.18s, complete read 0.451s, six bytes; confirms per-socket timeout does not ensure total deadline. Fixture has no external connections. See timeout-semantics-fixture.txt. Production timeout changes are not authorized/performed. Root cause of earlier URLError still unproven.

Proposed next offline hardening: supervise a result-only submission in a separate bounded worker, with no claim/execute/authorize route, no deletion or mutation of journal in worker, unchanged job/lease/payload, parent-held local locks, parent-only validation and journal deletion after verified matching ACK. Enforce monotonic overall retry budget and bounded subprocess wait, retain journal after any ambiguous timeout; stop request prevents subsequent retries/new claim, no force-kill of an executing production job. Do not equate termination of delivery-only worker with termination of original execution. Proposed initial engineering budget: 35s maximum per receipt submission worker, 3 attempts, overall 110s including 4s delays; measure and approve actual limits. If worker cannot be reaped or its outcome is ambiguous, block. Preserve preflight and current normal-tick fail-closed. Test DNS hang simulation, slow trickle read, TLS error, timeout after server commit, identical late ACK, stop during retry, lock exclusion, retained journal and startup recovery. Review ordinary child watchdog budget independently; do not blindly increase it or reduce exec cap without change approval. Do not claim hard absolute OS scheduling guarantees.
